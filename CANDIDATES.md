# Candidates — The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse

Brief: one line, max 140 characters, the funniest excuse a robot vacuum can give for missing one spot. Judge is a tired homeowner comparing entries pairwise and always picking the funnier one.

Scoring (0–10): how funny is it? / does it sound like a robot vacuum (sensors, dust, corners, cables, cat, dock/charging)? / does the punch land on the last beat? / short and tight?

Blind round — leaderboard and scores are hidden until results, so no top-10 study was possible; the strongest line was picked on the brief alone.

## Why #10 won

- **The missed spot is the most recognisable one:** dust under the couch is exactly where every robot vacuum fails and every owner looks.
- **Three-beat structure with the punch last:** setup (dust since 2019) → escalation (it pays rent now) → deadpan robot punchline (I respect tenants). The judge compares pairwise and the last beat is what sticks.
- **Deadpan robot voice, no human whining:** it treats dust as a tenant with rights — absurd logic delivered flat, which is the funniest register for a machine excuse.
- **Original angle in a 2,191-entry pool:** most entries will lean on the cat, the cable, or the dog. The "dust as a paying tenant" framing is rarer and more memorable.
- **88 characters:** short, tight, nothing to trim, comfortably inside the 140 limit.
